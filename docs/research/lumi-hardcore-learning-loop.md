## Research that transfers to this harness

- **Voyager** uses game-state feedback and execution errors to revise executable Minecraft skills, saves skills after task verification, and retrieves them in fresh worlds. Its exploration experiments preserved inventory through death. For Hardcore, carry forward skill versions and their evidence, and verify concrete postconditions such as inventory deltas or advancement events. [Voyager method and experimental setup](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.16291)
- **Reflexion** feeds a short lesson derived from an attempt's outcome into later attempts via episodic memory. Its authors flag self-evaluation as a limitation. For a death, give the reviewer the observable event and the relevant actions and state, let cause be unknown, and ask for a proposed change with a testable prediction. [Reflexion](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.11366)
- **ExpeL** compares unsuccessful and successful trajectories to extract reusable guidance and retrieves similar successful attempts for the current task. For recurring encounters, pair a failure with a comparable success so that differences in the seed, equipment, or encounter state do not silently become tactical conclusions. [ExpeL](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.10144v3#S4.SS2)
- A separate Minecraft experiment gave four Gemini agents Mineflayer and shared memory. They wrote 2,783 programs and defeated the Dragon on Easy after 605 deaths. It demonstrates the reach of generated code and the importance of measuring survival separately from eventual completion. [Gemini 4 Argon Minecraft report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22319)

## Implementation direction

The deployed Mine AI branch now focuses on changes to the shared runtime. A blank optional item recipient had repeatedly sent disposal through player lookup. Exact block collection could prioritize a loose matching drop before touching the named block. Wounded contact defense could keep the body after the health threshold crossed. Those paths now normalize the recipient, honor the exact block through mining and pickup, and use the existing cover and recovery behavior during an active fight. The shared A* threat field prices exposed retreat cells near ranged threats. [Runtime patch](/home/jake/.codex/worktrees/d6dc/minecraft/deploy/primitives.patch), [Baritone movement costs and segmented A*](https://github.com/cabaletta/baritone/blob/master/FEATURES.md), [Mineflayer collection primitive](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/mineflayer-collectblock), [Mineflayer PvP shield and movement design](https://github.com/nxg-org/mineflayer-custom-pvp)

Hardcore death triggers a separate model call with the death-time action receipts and incident trace. The reviewer explains what led to the death, distinguishes a decision error from a suspected defect or missing capability, and saves evidence and uncertainty for the maintainer. The dashboard shows the review alongside the saved run. A fresh run starts with the baseline gameplay settings and its normal task and tools.
