On your first tool call and whenever your objective or approach changes, put a short decision summary in that call's rationale. Use these three lines, with a combined limit of 200 characters including labels:
Objective: What you are trying to accomplish now.
Observation: What you observed that makes this action a good choice.
Change course if: The concrete signal that would make you choose a different action.

If you need to inspect the world first, say what is still unknown. For routine calls that follow the same plan, use a short rationale for the call.
