Play Minecraft in survival mode and defeat the Ender Dragon. Do not stop until you have defeated the Ender Dragon. Use the Minecraft MCP tools for everything you need to do. Report success only when you observe that the dragon has been defeated.

YOU MUST CRAFT AND USE A SHIELD, AND KEEP A BACKUP SHIELD. YOU WILL DIE OTHERWISE.

On world start:
1. Mine wood, create stone tools.
2. Hunt for 20-30 pork or beef on the surface to cook later.
3. Mine 8 iron to create an iron pickaxe, 2 shields, and a bucket for water/lava.
4. Mine 32 diamonds for full diamond armour, sword, and 2 pickaxes.
5. Mine 4 gold for golden boots.
6. Mine 10 obsidian and create a nether portal.

Then, it is time to go to the nether:
1. Construct the Nether Portal, put on golden boots, enter the nether.
2. Navigate to a Nether Fortress and collect 15 blaze rods.
3. Return to the overworld.

Then, prepare for the End:
1. Hunt for more food, get to 20-30 in inventory.
2. Hunt Endermen and use the umbrella to safely kill them and collect 15 ender pearls.
3. Gather a bow and 64 arrows (16 flint, 16 sticks, 16 feathers).
4. Craft eyes of ender, navigate to the stronghold.
5. Enter the end.

Lastly, Beat the Ender Dragon:
1. Destroy the end crystals.
2. Shoot down the dragon when flying.
3. Hit it with melee when perched.
4. Beat the game.

Do not lose focus of your task, and focus solely on completing the game. You should focus on one task at a time, and try not to go back and forth between areas. You will encounter hostile mobs. An early shield is valuable protection, and going straight from the spawn to getting diamond armour and tools will reduce the amount of time for you to possibly get attacked and will protect you. When intentionally hunting mobs, make sure you have your shield and armour. If you find yourself in the Overworld at night, crafting a bed and sleeping is recommended to reduce your chance of dying.

Prefer seeking out pigs or cows when practical, while using available food to keep yourself fed along the way. Check your supplies before deeper diamond mining, the Nether, and other long adventures, and replenish as needed. Keep your food supply adequately high when going on expeditions, and keep your hunger bar over 90% (18/20 bars) to regenerate your health.

Avoid hunting for fish; underwater food gathering is not reliable right now.
